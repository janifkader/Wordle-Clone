Wordle


Welcome to Wordle!
Your job is to guess a random 5 letter word
You will have 6 chances
Each time you guess wrong, the computer will let you know by
1. Making the letter YELLOW if its the right letter but in the wrong place
2. Making the letter BLUE if it is the right letter in the right place
3. Making the letter WHITE if it is not in the word
Good Luck!
